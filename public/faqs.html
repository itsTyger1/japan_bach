Getting Around

What is the best way to get around Tokyo?
Train and subway.
How do I get Suica on iPhone?
Wallet → + → Transit Card → Suica → Add money.
How do I get Suica on Android?
Mobile Suica may not work on many U.S. Android phones. If unsupported, get a physical Suica/PASMO after arrival.
When do trains stop?
Usually around midnight–1 AM.
What if we miss the last train?
Taxi or stay out until trains restart.
Should we use taxis often?
No. With 8 people, we usually need multiple cars.

Phone / Internet

What should I do for phone service?

Food

What food are we targeting?
Sushi, omakase, yakitori, ramen, wagyu, izakaya food, seafood, and market food.
What is omakase?
A chef-selected tasting menu where the chef chooses and serves a sequence of seasonal dishes, usually including several pieces of sushi.
When is omakase?
Oct 9 in Ginza.
Omakase budget?
Around $100 per person before drinks.
Will restaurants seat all 8 of us?
Not always. Some places may require reservations or splitting up.

Nightlife

  • Roppongi: upscale, international
What is Omoide Yokocho?
A tiny yakitori and izakaya alley in Shinjuku.
What is Golden Gai?
A maze of tiny bars in Shinjuku.
Can all 8 fit in Golden Gai bars?
Usually no.
Plan for Golden Gai?
Split into smaller groups, then regroup.
